Best for specific needs

Best for small kitchens

If counter space is at a premium, the Philips Smoke-Less TurboStar’s compact footprint and built-in smoke management make it the easiest fit. It tucks away neatly, requires less clearance than the Ninja or Breville, and won’t overwhelm a tight kitchen with lingering odors. The trade-off is a smaller cooking area, but for one or two people it’s more than sufficient. Our pick: Philips Smoke-Less TurboStar Indoor Grill.

Best for smoke-free apartment cooking

The number-one complaint with indoor grills is smoke. Philips’ TurboStar airflow design directs smoke downward and through a cooling channel, dramatically reducing the amount that escapes into your apartment. If you’ve set off a smoke alarm before, or your lease prohibits strong cooking odors, this is the grill to trust. It won’t eliminate all smoke (especially with fatty meats), but it handles better than any budget competitor. Our pick: Philips Smoke-Less TurboStar Indoor Grill.

Best for tech-savvy cooks who want precision

The Breville Smart Grill appeals to anyone who likes dialling in exact temperatures and relies on a meat probe for doneness. Its smart presets remember your favourite doneness levels, and the LCD screen gives real-time feedback. While it costs more than the Ninja, the level of control is unmatched in this price neighbourhood, ideal for those who cook meat often and hate guesswork. Our pick: Breville BGR820BXL Smart Grill.

Our verdict

For most people looking for a budget-friendly indoor grill that excels across the board, the Ninja Foodi Smart XL remains the clear winner: it cooks big batches, sears beautifully, and doubles as an air fryer. If smoke is your biggest concern, the Philips Smoke-Less TurboStar is the sensible alternative. And if you’re willing to spend a bit more for chef-grade precision, the Breville Smart Grill rewards the investment. All three models have strong reputations and will upgrade your indoor cooking without breaking the bank.
