Indoor grills have come a long way from the smoky, cramped countertop gadgets of years past. Choosing the best indoor grill means weighing how much smoke you’re willing to tolerate, how much counter space you have, and what you plan to cook most often. Some grills double as griddles or air fryers; others are laser-focused on delivering the best sear with minimal cleanup.
